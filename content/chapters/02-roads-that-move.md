By the end of the week, four roads had moved, and all of them leaned the same way: north-east, toward the pass, toward the Gate.

Mira plotted each shift on a sheet of thin tracing linen and laid the sheets one over another. The pattern, stacked like that, was impossible to un-see. It looked like water finding a crack. It looked like iron filings finding a magnet.

It looked, if she was honest, like something on the other side of the mountains was *pulling*.

She should have taken it straight to Master Odo. Instead — and she would turn this moment over for years, the way you turn a stone in your pocket — she took it to the archive, to check the oldest survey in the building.

The first map of the valleys was drawn 212 years ago, the year the Gate was sealed.

On it, in faded brown ink, the King's Road ran exactly where her road was trying to go.
