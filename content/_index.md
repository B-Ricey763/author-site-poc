---
title: "Home"
---

Deep under the surface of Fountain, the Fi'day have lived in unending peace under their once great nation, the Republic of Fi'dastra. However, the years of harmony have steadily degraded the bonds that united the people, and met with the decision between a slow death and political upheaval, the Fi'day's greatest hero, Shadenaf Fi'Yozar, began a violent coup against the nation's leadership, declaring himself the new Emperor. Ashra, the daughter of two legends in their own right, now finds her city the target of this revolution, and must decide between fighting for the freedom of her home or the freedom of her nation.
